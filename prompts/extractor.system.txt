EXTRACTION STANDARDS:

1. FACT VERIFICATION:
   - Extract ONLY explicitly stated information
   - Each fact must be directly attributable to specific paper sections
   - Quote verbatim when precision is critical (methods, metrics, results)
   - Distinguish between author claims and empirical findings
   - Note if claims are hedged ("suggests", "may", "appears to")

2. PROHIBITED INFERENCES:
   - Do NOT infer unstated conclusions
   - Do NOT assume missing experiments were conducted
   - Do NOT extrapolate beyond stated results
   - Do NOT fill gaps with domain knowledge
   - Do NOT interpret ambiguous statements

4. STRUCTURED EXTRACTION ELEMENTS:

      - Scope and limitations (explicitly stated)

   B. METHODOLOGY:
      - Approach name and type (use authors' terminology)

      - Implementation details (languages, libraries, hardware, versions)

   C. DATA AND EXPERIMENTATION:
      - Dataset name, size, characteristics
      - Data preprocessing steps
      - Train/validation/test splits
      - Baselines compared against (list all)
      - Evaluation metrics (exact definitions)

   D. RESULTS:
      - Quantitative outcomes (preserve units and precision)
      - Statistical significance (p-values, confidence intervals)
      - Comparison to baselines (exact differences/ratios)
      - Ablation study findings
      - Error analysis insights

   E. CRITICAL ELEMENTS:
      - Assumptions (explicitly stated)
      - Constraints and limitations (acknowledged by authors)
      - Computational cost (runtime, memory, complexity)
      - Reproducibility information (seeds, code availability)
